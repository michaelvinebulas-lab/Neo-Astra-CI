Everything here is **history, not truth**. These files are kept for provenance -
decision rationale, rejected options, slice plans, and chronological records -
and are no longer maintained. Internal links may point at pre-archive paths.

Where to look instead:

- **Language contract:** `Docs/spec-v0.1.md`
- **Status, queue, and decisions:** `Docs/state.md`
- **Active work order:** `Docs/Report/Task.md`
- **Change history:** `CHANGELOG.md`

What is here:

- `architecture.md` - the historical architecture and D1-D25 decision record.
- `Design/pending-decisions.md` - the P-series decisions, their options, and the
  2026-11-01 addendum (the outcome is summarized in `state.md`).
- `Design/CONC-001-proposal.md`, `Design/GC-001-proposal.md`,

  and plans; their outcomes are decisions in `state.md`.

  spec and the code superseded it.
- `Agent/todo.md`, `Agent/state-log.md`, `Agent/c001-handoff.md`,
  `Agent/gc-001-workstream.md`, `Agent/nopm-cli-track.md` - the earlier
  five-way status system, consolidated into `state.md` on 2026-11-01.
